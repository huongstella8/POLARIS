📘 Listening Strategy 1It is sometimes difficult to catch names and proper nouns when you listen. However, you can use the words around them (including collocations) to work out what they are (a person, a place, a thing). For example, if you hear "We stayed four nights at the Grand Plaza", the underlined words tell you that the Grand Plaza is a hotel.
📘 Listening Strategy 2 (Register)Being aware of formal register can help you identify the context. Formal terms: adjacent to (next to) · due to (because of) · prior to (before) · beverages (drinks) · to commence (to begin) · to depart (to leave) · to proceed to (to go to) · to purchase (to buy) · refreshments (food and drink) · to terminate (to end).
